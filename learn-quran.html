YOUR 10-MINUTE ROUTINE

A simple daily plan

  1. 2 min · Listen to a qualified teacher reading the sound.
  2. 3 min · Repeat the letter or vowel carefully.
  3. 3 min · Read a short example, slowly.
  4. 2 min · Review yesterday's lesson and note what felt difficult.
